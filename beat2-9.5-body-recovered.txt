INT. BRITANNIA CINEPLEX, 3090 CARLING AVENUE, OTTAWA — AUDITORIUM — EARLY EVENING (FLASHBACK — 1986, LATER THAT DAY)

A spaceship alarm tears through the packed house. YOUNG KEYS (10), young Tom Green likeness, freezes with one hand above an oversized, half-empty striped popcorn tub in his lap. No logo or lettering.

Throughout: slightly underexposed, with visible 35mm film grain.

ON SCREEN

David, in pyjamas, climbs liquid-chrome stairs into the mirrored ship. The hatch seals; alarms rise outside. Silvestri’s synth pulses.

MIDDLE ROW — CENTER SEAT

Blue-white projection catches Young Keys's face beneath his grown-out bowl cut; his half-unzipped windbreaker falls into shadow.

SLOW FRONTAL PUSH IN on Young Keys, from the screen side, at his eye level, beneath the projector’s dust cone.

MAX (ON SCREEN)
You wish to depart this hostile alien environment?

Young Keys mouths his answer a half-second before David.

YOUNG KEYS
(silently)
Yes. Go already.

David's reply disappears beneath the escape alarms.

ON SCREEN

The mirrored ship bursts into daylight. Silvestri's synth theme swells through the house.

MIDDLE ROW — CENTER SEAT

TIGHTER on Young Keys. The tub tips forward and settles in his lap, his fingers still hooked on the rim. Kernels spill down his jeans.

His grin fades. Under the ship’s escape alarms, he grips the front ends of the wooden armrests like controls, thumbs on top, eyes fixed on the escaping ship.

YOUNG KEYS
(silently, to himself)
I'm not giving it back.

TIGHTER STILL — his eyes fill the frame, the mirrored ship sliding across both irises. The screen flares white and wipes them out.

The music cuts dead.

MAX (ON SCREEN)
Compliance.
